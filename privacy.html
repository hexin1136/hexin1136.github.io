Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

ToolPick AI (“we”, “us”) operates this static website. This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit and how advertising partners may use cookies.

Information we collect

We do not require an account. Our site is static. Standard server or CDN logs (such as IP address, user agent, and requested URLs) may be collected by our hosting provider for security and reliability.

If you email us, we receive whatever you include in that message and use it only to respond.

Cookies and advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads based on prior visits to this site or other sites. Advertising, when it appears, is separate from our tool recommendations.

Learn how Google uses data when you use partners’ sites or apps:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You may also visit www.aboutads.info for more industry opt-out tools.

Affiliate links and cookies

Some pages include affiliate or partner links. If you click one of those links, the vendor or its tracking partner (for example an affiliate network) may set a cookie or similar technology so they can attribute a later sign-up or purchase to that click, for a limited period set by that partner.

Those cookies are used for referral tracking and commission payment, not to change our editorial recommendations. We do not control the affiliate partners’ cookies. You can block or delete them in your browser settings; if you do, a purchase may not be attributed to our link.

Advertising cookies from Google, when ads are shown, are described above and remain separate from affiliate tracking.

How we use information

  • To operate and secure the website
  • To understand aggregate traffic (if analytics are added later)
  • To respond to messages you send us
  • To support advertising delivered by third-party vendors, including Google, when ads are shown
  • To attribute referrals if you use affiliate or partner links on this site.

Data retention

Email correspondence is retained as long as needed to handle your request. Hosting logs follow the retention settings of our host/CDN.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
